KOReader Progress Viewer

Pick a folder with your ebooks. The page pairs each .epub with KOReader sidecar data (.sdr/metadata…lua) and optional .srt files, then shows your reading progress locally.

Ready. Use a button above to choose a folder. No files are uploaded; everything stays on your device.
Notes: File System Access works best in Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge) over HTTPS or localhost. Fallback uses the directory upload input. Progress is read-only.
